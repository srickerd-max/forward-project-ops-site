Merging Two Teams Into One Culture

Integrating two previously separate editorial teams into one unified group following a leadership change.

Client
A large financial media & affiliate marketing company
My role
Merger Lead
Work
Team Merger · Structure & Process Change · Team Leadership
Industry
Financial Services
Background

Two teams under the same company – one focused on educational content, one on transactional content – had operated separately for years, resulting in duplicated leadership costs, inconsistent culture, and no ability for either team to support the other during crunch periods.

The challenge

Design and execute a merger that normalized different workloads (including some content types that took far longer to produce than others) while securing buy-in from both teams and senior leadership.

What I did
  • {{ s.body }}
Results

The merger was substantially completed within roughly six months, successfully consolidating a 21-person team under one unified structure and culture.
